# Accordance

> Accordance (Agnetic Labs Inc.) is a San Francisco-based AI company building a frontier research platform for tax, audit, and accounting professionals. Its multi-agent AI helps CPAs and tax teams navigate regulations, conduct authoritative research, and deliver advice with visible reasoning and cited sources. Accordance is a product-led SaaS application with no public developer API at this time.
